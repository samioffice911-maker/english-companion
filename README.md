# English Companion

A personal AI English-speaking companion for a Bangla speaker. Talk naturally in English, Bangla, or both — it turns Bangla into natural English, corrects important mistakes, and talks back through your earbuds.

## Get the app
Download the newest APK: **[EnglishCompanion.apk](../../releases/latest/download/EnglishCompanion.apk)**

Every change pushed to `main` builds a new APK automatically (see the **Actions** tab).

## First-time setup
1. Install the APK (allow "install unknown apps" for your browser if asked).
2. Open the app → ⚙ Settings → paste your free Gemini API key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey → Save.
3. Choose a mode and tap **Start Companion**. Allow microphone and notifications.

## Modes
- **Companion** – quiet; speaks only for Bangla, important mistakes, or when you talk to it
- **Teacher** – corrects more and explains briefly
- **Conversation** – chats with you
- **Bangla Bridge** – turns your Bangla into English
- **Professional** – workplace English

## Voice commands
"Pause", "Continue", "Say it again", "Slower", "Faster", "Explain that", "Give me another way", "Let's practice", "Stop companion".

## Privacy
Audio is held in memory only, sent to Gemini, then discarded. Nothing is recorded to storage. Mistake memory and history can be turned off or deleted in Settings.
